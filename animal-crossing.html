Introduction

Animal Crossing New Horizons is a life simulation game that was made by Nintendo for the Nintendo Switch. Players can move to a deserted island and make their own community. The island design is totally up to the players(and Tom Nook).

Players can customize their homes, decorate the island, collect wildlife and are, and curate the villagers on the island. The game matches real-world time and seasons, which allows for different activities and events throughout the year based on the player's location and the current season.

Island Activities

There are many activities to take part in on the deserted island. Some activities help players earn Bells(money), while others allow players to customize the island or complete collections.

Fishing/Bug Catching

Players can catch fish and bugs on their islands. These creatures can be donated to the museum or sold for Bells. Most fish and bugs can only be caught during specific times of the day or year.

Island Customization

Players can decorate their islands with furniture, plant flowers and trees, construct bridges and inclines, and eventually unlock tools for changing the island's terrain and waterways.

Villager Interactions

Animal villagers live on the island with the player. Players can talk with villagers, exchange gifts, and participate in activities and seasonal celebrations. Players can also hunt for villagers when there is a vacancy on the island.
