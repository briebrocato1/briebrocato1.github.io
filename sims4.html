Welcome to The Sims 4

Basic Gameplay

The Sims 4 is a life simulation game where players can create characters, or Sims, and guide them through their everyday lives. Players can customize their Sims' appearances, personalities, relationships, careers, and homes.

Building and Decorating

In Build Mode, players can decororate the interiors and exteriors of their homes. Whether you prefer a small starter home or a large mansion, you can experiment with layouts, colors, and furniture.

Quick Tips

  • Start with a smaller home to manage money and get familiar with the game.
  • Keep an eye on your Sims' needs.
  • Explore different careers and hobbies.
